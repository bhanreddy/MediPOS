Simple user manual

A practical guide for medical shop owners and billing staff. Use MedPOS to prepare bills, record stock and check daily business.

Start here

1 Sign in

  1. Open the MedPOS website or app supplied by your shop administrator. Keep your internet connection on.
  2. Enter your Email address and Password. Select Sign in. Check that the correct shop name appears.
  3. If access or subscription is restricted, ask the shop owner or administrator for help.

2 Find your work area

  • Dashboard: see the shop overview and stock alerts.
  • Billing: create bills and find previous invoices.
  • Inventory: check medicine quantities, batches and expiry dates.
  • Purchases and Suppliers: record incoming stock and vendors.
  • Customers, Expenses and Reports: manage buyers, costs and business totals.

3 Before the first bill

  • Ask the owner to check shop details in Settings, including invoice details and the UPI ID if used.
  • Make sure your medicines and opening stock have been entered. Connect the receipt printer if you use one.
This guide follows the web app. On mobile, use Home, Stock, Sales and More; tap Home > New sale to start billing. Button names can differ by screen and account access.

Make a customer bill

4 Create a sale

  1. Open Billing > New invoice.
  2. Search for a medicine and select the correct result. Check its batch, expiry and available stock.
  3. Enter the quantity. Repeat for the other medicines. Check the price, discount if used, and final total.
  4. Choose the customer when needed. For Credit, select a saved customer.
  5. Choose Cash, UPI, Card or Credit. Check Amount received; for Credit, use Received now for any part payment.
  6. Select Confirm sale (or Confirm on a compact screen). For UPI, verify receipt in the shop payment account before confirming payment.
  7. Wait for the saved invoice or success message. Print the receipt if needed.

Understand the payment

  • Cash: check the money received and return the displayed change.
  • UPI or Card: verify that payment succeeded. A displayed QR code alone does not confirm payment.
  • Credit: the unpaid amount becomes the customer's balance due. The app checks the customer's credit limit.

5 Find or reprint a bill

  1. Open Billing and search for the invoice or customer; use the date filters if needed.
  2. Use the view icon to inspect the invoice, or the printer icon to reprint it.
If printing fails after the invoice is saved, reprint the existing invoice. Before retrying an uncertain sale, check Billing to avoid creating a duplicate.

Manage stock and purchases

6 Check or add stock

  1. Open Inventory. Search by medicine or batch. Read In stock, Expiry and Status.
  2. To enter opening or direct stock, choose Add batch (F6). Search for the medicine; use the new medicine option only when it is missing.
  3. Enter the batch number, expiry, quantity, cost, MRP and sales rate shown on the form. Check the unit carefully, for example a strip or a tablet.
  4. Save the batch and confirm that it appears in Inventory.

7 Record a supplier purchase

  1. If the vendor is new, open Suppliers > Add supplier. Enter the company name and available contact details, then Save supplier.
  2. Open Purchases > New purchase. Choose the supplier; enter the supplier invoice number and received date.
  3. Choose Add item (F6). Select the medicine and enter batch, expiry, quantity, cost and MRP. Add each item.
  4. Compare the entries and total with the supplier bill. Select Save purchase (F12). Check for Purchase saved and verify the stock.

Watch stock alerts

  • Check low stock and expiring stock each day. Reorder or Shortbook helps track medicines to buy.
  • Keep expired or damaged stock apart and ask the responsible person to handle it.
Record each incoming batch once. Do not enter the same supplier stock through both Add batch and New purchase, because both add stock.

Customers and daily records

8 Add a customer

  1. Open Customers > Add customer.
  2. Enter the name and a valid 10-digit mobile number. Add an email and credit limit if needed.
  3. Save the customer. Select this saved record during billing when giving credit. Check Balance due in invoice details.

9 Record an expense

  1. Open Expenses > Record expense.
  2. Choose the category and enter the amount, date, payment method and a clear description.
  3. Select Save expense. Check the new entry in the list.

10 View a report

  1. Open Reports and choose Sales report, Purchase report, Expense report, GST report or Profit & loss.
  2. Set From and To dates for the period you want.
  3. Select Generate report and read the preview. If you change the report or dates, generate it again.

Mobile navigation

  • Stock opens medicine stock; Sales opens past bills.
  • More contains Patients, Expenses, Reports, Accounting, Purchases, Suppliers and Shortbook.
  • Open Settings from Home when needed. Mobile forms may use different labels from the web steps.
Totals marked 'on this page' cover only the visible list page. Use Reports with the correct date range for period totals.

Daily routine and help

Start and finish the day

  • Start: sign in, check the shop name, internet, printer and stock alerts.
  • During the day: record every sale, purchase and expense; check each save result.
  • Close: generate today's sales and expense reports. Compare recorded cash, UPI and card receipts with the actual collections; review credit dues.
  • Sign out using the account's logout control, especially on a shared device.

If something goes wrong

  • Cannot sign in: check the email, password and internet. Ask the administrator if access remains blocked.
  • Medicine missing: check the spelling and Inventory. Ask the stock manager to add a missing medicine or batch.
  • Insufficient stock or no sellable batch: check quantity, batch and expiry. Correct the stock record or record a genuine inward purchase.
  • Save seems stuck or internet drops: reconnect and check the relevant list before retrying. Treat the entry as complete only after a saved record is visible.
  • Printer fails: check the printer connection and paper. Reprint the saved bill from Billing.
  • Wrong saved bill or return: note the invoice number and ask the owner or authorized staff to handle the correction or return.

Useful words

  • Batch: a particular lot of medicine. Expiry: the last usable date shown for that batch.
  • MRP: maximum retail price. Credit / Balance due: money the customer still owes.
When asking for help, give the screen name, invoice or batch number, time and exact error message. Keep passwords and customer details private.
